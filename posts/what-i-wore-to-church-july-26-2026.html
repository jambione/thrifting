What I Wore to Church - July 26, 2026

kara July 26, 2026

Church and work are normally the only places that I end up dressing properly for, unless I'm going out to dinner or something. Honestly, we should all take a little more time to dress up because it really improves your mood the tidier you look!

To church today I wore:

Denim jacket, Wrangler, $12.65, ThredUp. This was a final sale item I got lucky on. It's soft and comfortable.

T-shirt, Quince, not thrifted, $13. Quince t-shirts for the win. Best t-shirts I've got, hands down.

Skirt, Boden, $20.90, ThredUp. I love this embroidered linen skirt so much. But unfortunately, it's a little too big. I was pulling it up the entire time.

Shoes, Spring Step L'Artiste, $37.99, ThredUp. These aren't the most comfortable L'Artiste shoes I've ever worn, but they're not bad. Ankle straps are not my favorite.

Handbag, Longchamp, gift from my mom. Workhorse in a pretty color.

The cross I'm wearing was given to me by my grandparents when I made my first communion in 1983-ish.

Total outfit cost: $84.54.
