What I almost wore to a wedding

kara October 26, 2026

It's sort of a funny thing, how to prepare for something that you are expecting to happen but doesn't at the last minute. My mom asked me months ago to be her "plus one" to a wedding for her neighbors' daughter. My first reaction was, "Oh, no way. Going to a wedding where I don't know anyone except my mom and her neighbors is NOT this introvert's idea of a good time." Then I thought, well, this might be a good opportunity to wear this gorgeous dress I snapped up on ThredUp for $42. 

Chiara Boni La Petite Robe is a brand I'd never heard of, but with the help of AI, I was able to track down the specific dress and discovered that new a couple of years ago, it was being sold at Saks Fifth Avenue for $750. Wow! I was thrilled I got it for $42. It fits like an absolute glove, and is seriously the classiest thing I have in my closet right now.

Shoes were a gamble. I haven't ever bought Thierry Rabotin shoes before, these are kitten heeled satin shoes with leather trim. They're pretty comfortable, but I wish I'd gotten a 40 instead of a 40.5. I forgot that shoes fit differently in stockings than they do with bare feet.

The bag was ultra adorable. It looks JUST LIKE MY DOG! Well, just like her if she dressed up in sunglasses and a fancy dress. I saw this new with tags on Poshmark for $70. I was like, Maeve for Anthropologie, on Poshmark $70 seems like an awful lot... until I realized that people collect these bags and most of the ones selling on Poshmark and eBay were over $100! The Maeve Fiona bags are typically about $100 on Anthropologie's website, but these were on sale for a very limited time for about $75 until they sold out. So even though it was expensive, it was new with tags, and I probably would not have paid much more if I'd bought it new. Still, it was a brilliant find and I love it because it looks like my dog.

Then came the agonizing what to wear over it since it is a late October wedding. I searched everywhere. I bought a sweater wrap thing on ThredUp, final sale, for $10 but it looked like a blanket. In fact, it's so much like a blanket I'll probably donate it to Goodwill. After visiting 5 thrift/consignment shops, I finally ended up buying a faux fur trimmed cape-thing at Marshall's for $17.

I spent most of the morning having my hair cut, then repairing the pearl necklace I was going to wear. I finally got dressed, and just as I was putting on my stockings, my mom called to tell me she felt sick and didn't want to risk going to the wedding.

Whatever. I left the dress on and took pictures anyway. I'll probably wear it out to dinner for my son's birthday in November.
